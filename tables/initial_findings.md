# Initial descriptive findings

These are the default **representative trust index**, **linear models**, **original weights**, adjusted for **age, gender and education**. Associations contrast predicted agreement at trust 1 versus trust 0 on the normalized 0–1 response scale. All outcomes retain their statement-agreement direction.

The most consistent positive association is with democracy’s preferability. This does not carry over uniformly to the other attitudes: trust also often has a positive association with agreement that a strong leader may bend the rules. These attitudes should therefore remain separate rather than be interpreted as interchangeable measures of democratic support.

For democracy’s preferability, the default study association ranges from about −0.133 in Turkiye to +0.440 in Taiwan. The Great Britain estimate is +0.096 (95% interval +0.033 to +0.160). These are full-range trust contrasts, not changes for one response-category step or one standard deviation. Differences in separate point estimates do not themselves establish significant differences between studies.

The default pre-election cabinet-ideology comparison retains 15 studies at 80% coverage. Its second-stage slope is −0.0227 per cabinet ideology point (95% interval −0.0533 to +0.0079). This is imprecise and includes zero. Changing timing, coverage, trust measure, or outcome may change the comparison. It is not an identified causal moderation estimate.

France has no complete cases for the default demographic adjustment. This exclusion is explicit; its unadjusted models remain available. All counts above are pointwise and exploratory, with no multiplicity correction. They describe this supplied advance release and these specifications, not a universal relationship. The full original complex survey designs cannot be reconstructed from the supplied harmonized file.
